# NNNN. Short title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** Who made the call

## Context

What situation forced a decision? What constraints were in play — technical,
practical, or about who maintains this? Write enough that someone arriving in a
year understands the pressure without having been here.

## Decision

What we are doing, stated plainly and in the present tense.

## Consequences

What follows from this, good and bad. Name the costs explicitly. An ADR that
only lists benefits is not recording a decision, it is advertising one.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table, and why it was not chosen. This is the section that
stops the same debate being reopened every six months.

## Revisit when

The specific condition that should make someone reconsider. "When X ships",
"when we reach 1.0", "if Y stops being true". If no such condition exists, say
so — some decisions are permanent and it helps to know which.
